# Acca Finder: Explained

**By Everyone's A Winner**
*Estimated read time: 4 minutes*

---

## What an acca is

An **accumulator** (or "acca") is one bet that links several selections together. Every leg has to win for the bet to win — and because the odds multiply, the combined odds climb fast.

> Two legs at 1.80 and 1.90 combine to **3.4**. Four legs at similar prices can easily pass **10.0**.

Bookmakers push accas hard because most of them lose. Matched bettors use them differently: they turn a **free bet** into a near-certain cash profit by laying each leg.

---

## Why we lay leg-by-leg

You can't lay a whole acca on an exchange — Betfair only lets you lay individual outcomes. So we lay the acca **one leg at a time, in order**:

1. Place the acca at the bookmaker using your free bet.
2. **Lay leg 1** on the exchange.
3. If leg 1 wins, **lay leg 2**. If leg 1 loses, you stop — you've already banked the profit.
4. Repeat until the acca finishes.

The lay stakes are sized so that, wherever the acca dies (or if it wins), you walk away with roughly the **same guaranteed profit**.

---

## The "stake not returned" (SNR) rule

Most free bets are **stake-not-returned**: the bookmaker pays only your *profit*, not your original stake.

So a £10 free bet at combined odds of **4.0** returns **£30** (your £10 stake isn't added back) — that's £10 × (4.0 − 1).

Every number in the Acca Finder already applies this SNR rule, plus the standard **5% exchange commission** — the same conventions as the rest of the site.

---

## How to read a card

- **Combined odds** — the product of every leg's back odds.
- **Guaranteed retained profit** — the cash you keep, whether the acca wins or any leg loses.
- **Legs (lay in order)** — each leg's back odds vs lay odds, plus the lay stake and liability for that step.
- **Acca return** — what the free bet pays if the whole acca wins.
- **Liability** — the most you'd have out at any one time on the exchange.
- **Retention** — what share of the free bet's value you keep.

---

## One important honesty rule

**Same-match combinations can't be laid.** If two legs come from the *same* fixture (a "bet builder" / "same-game multi"), you cannot hedge them leg-by-leg on the exchange — so we never show those, and never pretend to. Every acca here combines legs from **different matches**.

---

## Honest expectations

- **Lay leg-by-leg in order, and only after each leg settles.** Laying everything upfront is a different (riskier) strategy and the numbers won't match.
- **Odds drift.** Prices move between when you place the back bet and the lay. Re-check each leg's lay odds just before you lay.
- **Liquidity.** Small markets may not have enough money on the exchange to take your full lay stake. The lay odds shown are real but availability can vary.
- **This is not guaranteed income.** The maths locks in a profit *if* you execute each lay correctly. Human error and moving odds are real risks.

---

## Responsible betting

- You must be **18 or over** to bet in the UK and Ireland.
- **Check your liability** before every lay — that's your real risk on the exchange.
- Matched betting minimises risk, but it is not zero-risk. Treat it as a side hustle, not a wage.
- If betting stops being fun, step away and use **GamStop** or **BeGambleAware.org**.
