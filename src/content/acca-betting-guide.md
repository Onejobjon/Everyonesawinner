# Acca Matched Betting Explained: A Beginner's Guide

**By Everyone's A Winner**
*Estimated read time: 6 minutes*

---

## The Short Answer

An **acca** (short for accumulator) is one bet made of several picks that all have to win. Bookmakers push them hard — and hand out acca-specific free bets and insurance offers — because they're great for the bookmaker.

Matched betting on an acca means **laying each leg one at a time** on Betfair, in order, instead of laying the whole thing at once. Done right, you turn an acca free bet into a **guaranteed slice of cash** — roughly 70–80% of its value — whichever way the games go.

---

## What an Acca Is

A normal bet is one pick: "Arsenal to win."

An **accumulator** combines several picks into a single bet. Every pick — called a **leg** — must win for the acca to pay out. If even one leg loses, the whole acca loses.

The odds **multiply**, which is why accas look tempting:

| Leg | Odds |
|-----|------|
| Match 1 — team to win | 2.00 |
| Match 2 — team to win | 2.00 |
| Match 3 — team to win | 2.00 |
| **Acca total** | **8.00** |

Three "even-money" picks become one 8.00 bet. A £20 stake would return £160 — but only if **all three** come in. That "all or nothing" nature is exactly what matched betting removes.

---

## Why Bookmakers Push Accas

Accas are the bookmaker's favourite bet to sell, for two reasons:

1. **The margin compounds.** Every leg carries the bookmaker's built-in edge. Multiply several legs together and that edge stacks up fast.
2. **They attach offers to accas.** The most common are:

| Offer | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| **Acca free bet** | A free bet that can only be used on a 3+ leg acca. |
| **Acca insurance** | If exactly one leg lets you down, your stake is refunded (usually as a free bet). |
| **Acca boost** | A percentage added to your acca winnings (e.g., "25–70% boost on 3+ selections"). |

These offers are where the matched-betting value is. You're not trying to predict the games — you're converting the offer into a known amount of cash.

## Key Terms in Plain Words

| Term | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| **Acca (accumulator)** | One bet combining several selections. All must win. |
| **Leg** | One selection inside an acca (e.g., "Arsenal to win"). |
| **Acca odds** | The multiplied odds of every leg together. |
| **SNR** | "Stake not returned" — a free bet pays only the *winnings*, not the stake. |
| **Lay each leg** | Lay one leg at a time on the exchange, rather than laying the whole acca. |
| **Liability** | The money you'd pay out on a lay bet if that leg wins. It's your risk on that leg. |

---

## The Lay-Each-Leg Strategy

You can't usually lay a whole 4-leg acca in one go — exchanges don't offer a liquid market for "these four specific teams all to win." So instead, you **lay the legs one at a time, in order**:

1. **Lay leg 1** on Betfair before its kick-off.
2. **If leg 1 wins**, lay leg 2 before *its* kick-off.
3. **If leg 2 wins**, lay leg 3 — and so on.

The rule is simple: **only lay the next leg once the previous one has won.** If a leg loses, the acca is dead, so you stop — and, because you laid that leg, you've already banked your profit.

The big advantage is capital: you only ever need exchange funds for **one leg at a time**, not the whole acca. The lay stakes do grow as legs win (more on that below), but you're never on the hook for all of it upfront.

## A Worked £20 Example

You have a **£20 free bet** (SNR — you don't get the stake back, only the winnings). You use it on a 3-leg acca:

- Three legs, each at **2.00**. Acca odds = **8.00**.
- If all three win, the free bet pays £20 × (8.00 − 1) = **£140**.
- You lay each leg at **2.05** on Betfair (5% commission).

You lay them in sequence, like this:

| Step | Leg | Back odds | Lay odds | Lay stake | Liability |
|------|-----|-----------|----------|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | Match 1 | 2.00 | 2.05 | £15.79 | £16.58 |
| 2 | Match 2 | 2.00 | 2.05 | £33.25 | £34.91 |
| 3 | Match 3 | 2.00 | 2.05 | £70.00 | £73.50 |

Each lay stake is sized so that whether that leg wins or loses, you finish in the same place — that's what locks in the £15. Check it by hand: add up the three liabilities (£16.58 + £34.91 + £73.50 = **£125.00**), and £140 − £125 = **£15.00**.

**The result:** if all three legs win, the free bet pays £140 and your three lay liabilities total £125.00 — leaving **£15.00**. If a leg loses at any point, that leg's lay wins and you still finish about **£15.00** up.

Whichever way it goes, you've kept **£15.00 of the £20 free bet — that's 75%**.

> Notice the lay stakes grow. Each time a leg wins, more of the acca's value is locked in, so the next lay is bigger. The £70 lay on leg 3 is only placed if legs 1 and 2 have *already won* — before that, you only needed the £15.79 for leg 1.

---

## What Happens When a Leg Loses

This is the part people worry about, but it's the strategy's safety net:

- **Leg 1 loses** → the acca is dead, but your leg 1 lay wins £15.79 (minus 5% = £15.00). You stop there. Legs 2 and 3 are never laid. **You're £15.00 up.**
- **Leg 1 wins, leg 2 loses** → you've paid leg 1's liability (£16.58) but leg 2's lay wins £31.59. Net: **about £15.00 up.**
- **Legs 1 and 2 win, leg 3 loses** → you've paid two liabilities (£51.49) but leg 3's lay wins £66.50. Net: **about £15.00 up.**

Because each leg is laid as you go, a losing leg doesn't wipe you out — it *pays out*. That's the whole point.

## What You Can't Lay: Same-Match Bet Builders

There's one important exception, and it's easy to trip on.

A **same-match bet builder** (also called a "same-game multi") combines several selections from the **same match** — for example, "Arsenal to win **and** over 2.5 goals **and** Saka to score."

You **can't** matched-bet these, because:

- There's no exchange market for a same-match combination, so there's nothing to lay.
- The selections are linked — if Arsenal win 3–1, several of your picks likely land together. You can't cover them with separate lays.

**Rule of thumb:** only use accas where every leg is a **separate match** you can lay individually on the exchange. If the bet builder says "same game," skip it for matched betting.

## Honest Expectations

- **Aim to retain 70–80% of a free bet's value.** The £20 example above keeps £15 (75%). That's the realistic target, not 100%.
- **Accas can beat the 70–80% rule of thumb.** When every leg is laid at a tight gap, laying leg-by-leg can retain more — the Acca Finder shows the exact retained profit for every plan, so judge each acca by its own numbers.
- **The gap matters.** The closer the lay odds are to the back odds, the more you keep. Wide gaps quietly eat your profit.
- **You need exchange funds for the later legs.** If the acca survives to leg 3, be ready to cover that £73.50 liability.
- **Odds move between legs.** By the time leg 2 kicks off, leg 2's lay odds may have shifted. Re-check before you lay each leg.
- **This is not guaranteed income.** Human error, changing odds, or thin exchange liquidity can all cut into results. Double-check every number before confirming.

## Responsible Betting

- You must be **18 or over** to bet in the UK and Ireland.
- **Never stake more than you can afford to lose**, including the liability on lay bets — always check what you'd owe before you place one.
- Matched betting is designed to minimise risk, but it is not zero-risk. Treat it as a side hustle, not a wage.
- If betting stops being fun or starts to feel like a problem, step away and use tools like **GamStop** or **BeGambleAware.org**.
