# Lay Betting Explained: A Beginner's Guide

**By Everyone's A Winner**
*Estimated read time: 5 minutes*

---

## The Short Answer

A **lay bet** is betting **against** something happening. Instead of betting that a team *will* win, you're betting that they *won't* win.

Lay "Arsenal to win" and you win if Arsenal **draw or lose**. It's the missing half of matched betting — the half that makes your profit certain, no matter the result.

---

## What a Betting Exchange (Betfair) Is

A normal bookmaker takes your bet directly. If you back Arsenal to win, you're betting against the bookmaker's own money.

A **betting exchange** like Betfair is different — it's a **marketplace** where real people bet against each other. When you place a lay bet, another punter (or several) takes the other side. This means **you act as the bookmaker**: you're the one offering the bet, and you get to keep the other person's stake if the outcome you laid *doesn't* happen.

That's exactly what makes lay bets possible. Betfair takes a small cut for running the exchange — more on that below.

---

## Key Terms in Plain Words

| Term | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| **Back bet** | A normal bet that something *will* happen (e.g., "Arsenal to win"). |
| **Lay bet** | A bet that something *won't* happen (e.g., "Arsenal *not* to win"). |
| **Back odds** | The odds a bookmaker gives you to back an outcome. |
| **Lay odds** | The odds you accept when you lay an outcome on the exchange. |
| **Lay stake** | The amount the other person is betting against you. You collect this if your lay wins. |
| **Liability** | The money **you** would pay out if the outcome you laid actually happens. It's your risk on a lay bet. |
| **Commission** | Betfair's fee — it keeps **5%** of any winnings you make on the exchange. |

The one to truly understand is **liability** — on a lay bet, that's the amount you'd owe the winner, and it's your real risk.

## Why Lay Bets Matter for Matched Betting

Matched betting works by covering **both** sides of the same outcome:

1. **Back** an outcome at a bookmaker.
2. **Lay** the exact same outcome on Betfair.

Now every possible result is covered — if your back bet wins, your lay bet loses, and vice versa. You don't need to predict the result at all; the two bets just need to be roughly the same size.

The result is a **small, controlled loss** (or occasionally a tiny profit) instead of a gamble. That loss is the **"qualifying loss"** — the normal price of unlocking a bookmaker's free bet. The real money comes later, from the free bets themselves.

---

## A Worked £20 Example

Here's exactly how the numbers play out. You:

- **Back** £20 on a team to win at odds of **1.50** (decimal odds) at a bookmaker.
- **Lay** that same team on Betfair at odds of **1.55**, with a **lay stake of £20.00**.

**Your liability** on the lay bet is:

> £20.00 × (1.55 − 1) = **£11.00**

So £11.00 is the most you can lose on the exchange side. Now look at both outcomes:

| Scenario | Bookmaker (back) | Betfair (lay) | Net result |
|----------|------------------|---------------|------------|
| **Back wins** | You win £10 profit (£20 × 0.50) | You pay out £11 liability | **−£1.00** |
| **Back loses** | You lose your £20 stake | You win £20 (minus 5% = £19) | **−£1.00** |

**Whichever way it goes, you lose about £1.00.** That's your qualifying loss — the small, predictable cost of earning the free bet, instead of risking the full £20 on a guess.

## Honest Expectations

- **Qualifying losses are normal.** Losing £0.50–£1 to unlock a free bet is expected — it's the cost of doing business, not a mistake.
- **The real profit comes from free bets.** You typically keep 70–80% of each free bet's value after the lay. A £30 free bet usually converts to roughly £20–£25 cash.
- **Betting involves risk.** Human error, changing odds, or exchange liquidity can all eat into results. Always double-check your numbers before confirming.
- **Always check the offer's T&Cs** — minimum odds, time limits, and payment method rules vary by bookmaker.
- **This is not guaranteed income.** We give you the tools and the maths, but results depend on you placing the bets correctly.

## Responsible Betting

- You must be **18 or over** to bet in the UK and Ireland.
- **Never stake more than you can afford to lose**, including the liability on lay bets — always check what you'd owe before you place one.
- Matched betting is designed to minimise risk, but it is not zero-risk. Treat it as a side hustle, not a wage.
- If betting stops being fun or starts to feel like a problem, step away and use tools like **GamStop** or **BeGambleAware.org**.
